Our Story 🤍

Written long before we even realised it.

Some love stories begin with a first conversation. Ours began with a glance.

“Where paths crossed in the quietest moment.”

Chapter 01  ·  The Glance

Love at first sight — although neither was brave enough to speak!

“There was an instant connection, a quiet, undeniable feeling that something about this person was different.”

Almost a year and a half ago, Lizaan and Rishen met for the first time at a funeral, where they were introduced by one of Lizaan's family members.

There was an instant spark — that rare feeling where the rest of the room fades away. It was true love at first sight — although neither of them was quite brave enough to actually say a word to the other in that moment 😂!

“Two people who became each other's favourite hello.”

Chapter 02  ·  The Conversation

The conversation that never happened in person began over text.

“What started as simple messages quickly became hours of laughing, sharing dreams, and falling completely in love.”

A few days later, Rishen still couldn't get Lizaan out of his mind. So, with a little help from the same family member, he got her number. And just like that, the conversation that was meant to be began.

Messages turned into late-night phone calls, inside jokes, and countless memories. Two people who were once too shy to speak became each other's favourite good morning and favourite good night.

“Late-night conversations, inside jokes & laughter.”

Chapter 03  ·  Faith & Forever

Like iron sharpens iron — to encourage, strengthen and love.

“We believe God brought our paths together at exactly the right time, writing our story long before we even realised it.”

Since then, we've shared so many unforgettable moments together — adventures, laughter, milestones, and quiet days made special simply by being with each other.

Like iron sharpens iron. We are committed to encouraging, strengthening, and bringing out the very best in one another.

Now, as we prepare to become husband and wife, we're filled with gratitude for everything that brought us here, and excitement for every chapter God has planned for us.

❦

“Therefore what God has joined together,
let no one separate.”

And so, what began with a glance, grew through messages, and blossomed into a love we never expected, is becoming forever 🤍.
