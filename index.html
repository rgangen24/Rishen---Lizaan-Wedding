Our Story

Written long before
we realised it.

01
A Glance

Two people, one room, a thousand unspoken words.

It was the most unexpected of settings — a gathering to say goodbye to someone loved. Lizaan and Rishen were introduced by one of Lizaan's family members that day, and something quiet and unmistakable passed between them. Neither had the courage to say much. There was a shyness to it — an awareness that this moment was different, even if neither could yet explain why.

Sometimes the most significant chapters begin in the smallest rooms, in the gentlest silence.

02
A Conversation

Love that began with three small words: "Can I have…?"

A few days after that quiet afternoon, Rishen found the courage to ask for Lizaan's number — with a little help from the same family member who had made the introduction. And so it began: not with a grand gesture, but with a message.

What followed was something rare. Good-morning messages that set the tone for entire days. Late-night conversations that ran past midnight and still felt too short. Inside jokes that belong only to them. Shared dreams whispered across phone screens in the hours when the world had gone still.

03
A Love Shaped by Faith

Friendship, faith, and the quiet certainty that God had planned this.

Their love was never only romantic — it was built on a foundation of deep friendship. They encouraged each other. They brought out something better in one another. Through adventures and milestones, through ordinary days and extraordinary moments, they grew into two people who were stronger together than apart.

They both believe, with wholehearted conviction, that God placed them in each other's lives at exactly the right time — that their paths were being woven together long before that afternoon, long before the first message was sent, long before either of them dared to hope.

Highberry Vineyards & Venue

Set against the dramatic contours of the Helderberg Mountain and surrounded by working vineyards, Highberry is one of the Cape Winelands' most beautifully considered event spaces. It is a place of natural grandeur softened by warmth — exactly the kind of setting where a love story like Rishen and Lizaan's deserves to be celebrated.

Somerset West sits in the heart of the Cape Winelands, less than an hour from Cape Town International Airport. The region offers world-class wine estates, mountain scenery, and some of the most generous hospitality the Cape has to give.
