Love Story

Our Story

Written long before we even realised it.

Chapter 01  ·  The Glance

Love at first sight — although neither was brave enough to speak!

“There was an instant connection, a quiet, undeniable feeling that something about this person was different.”

Almost a year and a half ago, Lizaan and Rishen met for the first time at a funeral, where they were introduced by one of Lizaan's family members. There was an instant spark — that rare feeling where the rest of the room fades away. It was true love at first sight, although neither was brave enough to speak in that moment!

Chapter 02  ·  The Conversation

The conversation that never happened in person began over text.

“What started as simple messages quickly became hours of laughing, sharing dreams, and falling completely in love.”

A few days later, Rishen still couldn't get Lizaan out of his mind. So, with a little help from the same family member, he got her number. Messages turned into late-night phone calls, inside jokes, and countless memories. Two people who were once too shy to speak became each other's favourite part of every day.

Chapter 03  ·  Faith & Forever

Like iron sharpens iron — to encourage, strengthen and love.

“We believe God brought our paths together at exactly the right time, writing our story long before we even realised it.”

Since then, their relationship has flourished through deep friendship, faith, unwavering encouragement, and bringing out the very best in one another. Now, as they prepare to exchange lifelong vows, they are filled with profound gratitude and joy for every chapter God has written ahead.
