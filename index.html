Our Story

“An instant spark across the room.”

Chapter 01  ·  The Glance

Love at first sight — although neither was brave enough to speak!

“There was an instant connection, a quiet, undeniable feeling that something about this person was different.”

Almost a year and a half ago, Lizaan and Rishen met for the first time at a funeral, where they were introduced by one of Lizaan's family members.

There was an immediate spark — that rare moment where everything else in the room gently fades into the background. It was true love at first sight, although neither was quite brave enough to say a single word to the other in that moment!

“Two people who became each other's favourite hello.”

Chapter 02  ·  The Conversation

The conversation that never happened in person began over text.

“What started as simple messages quickly became hours of laughing, sharing dreams, and falling completely in love.”

A few days later, Rishen still couldn't get Lizaan out of his mind. With a little help from the same family member, he got her number. And just like that, the conversation that was always meant to happen began.

Simple texts quickly grew into hours of talking, good-morning messages, late-night phone calls, inside jokes, and countless memories. Two people who were once too shy to speak became each other's favourite part of every day.

“Adventures, laughter & shared milestones.”

Chapter 03  ·  Faith & Forever

Like iron sharpens iron — to encourage, strengthen and love.

“We believe God brought our paths together at exactly the right time, writing our story long before we even realised it.”

Since then, their relationship has flourished through deep friendship, faith, unwavering encouragement, and bringing out the very best in one another.

Now, as they prepare to exchange lifelong vows and become husband and wife, they are filled with profound gratitude for every step that led here, and anticipation for every chapter God has written ahead.

“Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate.”

Matthew 19 : 6

And so, what began with a glance and grew through messages is becoming forever.
