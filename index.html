Our Story

Some love stories begin with a first conversation. Ours began with a glance.

R ♥ L

Almost a year and a half ago, Lizaan and Rishen met for the first time at a funeral, where they were introduced by one of Lizaan’s family members. There was an instant connection, a quiet, undeniable feeling that something about this person was different. It was love at first sight - although neither of them was brave enough to actually say a word to the other 😂!

A few days later, Rishen still couldn’t get Lizaan out of his mind. So, with a little help from the same family member, he got her number. And just like that, the conversation that never happened in person began over text.

What started as simple messages quickly became hours of talking, learning about one another, laughing, sharing dreams, and slowly falling completely in love. Somewhere between the good morning messages, late night conversations, inside jokes and countless memories, two people who were once too shy to speak became each other’s favourite good morning and favourite good night.

Since then, we’ve shared so many beautiful moments together, from ordinary days that became special simply because we were together, to adventures, laughter, memories and milestones that we’ll treasure forever. Through it all, our love has continued to grow, and we’ve come to realise that the best part of life is having someone beside you to share it all with.

Like iron sharpens iron. We believe we are meant to encourage, strengthen and bring out the best in one another.

We believe our story was never simply a coincidence. We believe God brought our paths together at exactly the right time and has been writing our story long before we even realised it. He has blessed us with a love that has grown through friendship, faith, laughter and the promise of a lifetime together.

Now, as we prepare to become husband and wife, we’re filled with gratitude for everything that brought us here, and excitement for everything that is still to come.

We can’t wait to spend the rest of our lives choosing each other, building our home, creating beautiful memories, growing in faith, and walking hand in hand through every chapter God has planned for us.

“Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate.”

Matthew 19:6

And so, what began with a glance, grew through messages, and blossomed into a love we never expected, is becoming forever.

Our greatest adventure is just beginning.
